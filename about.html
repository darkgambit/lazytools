About LazyTools

A small, honest collection of free browser tools — built to be fast, private and permanently free.

What is this?

LazyTools is a growing library of everyday utilities — calculators, converters and generators such as Age Calculator, Percentage Calculator, Compound Interest Calculator, BMI Calculator, Word Counter, Invoice Generator and more. Every tool runs 100% in your browser: nothing you type is sent to a server, which is both faster for you and safer for your data.

How we make money (full disclosure)

Two ways, and only two ways:

1. Display advertising. Banner slots on these pages are served by third-party ad networks. They may use cookies as described in our privacy policy.

2. Affiliate links. Occasionally we may recommend a product or service and earn a small commission if you sign up. Recommendations are marked, and commissions never change what we recommend.

We do not sell your data, we do not have accounts, and we do not charge for any tool. If that model ever changes, this page changes with it.
